## Confirmed breaks

### B1 (P1) — a receipt rendered under a refreshed grant can never settle; the ticket is held forever and keeps spending the free budget

- Repro: `ATTACK L1 (live DB)` and `ATTACK L1b (live DB)` in the attack file (command above).
- Observed: `issue_offline_grant()` re-issues the installation's outstanding tickets under the
  next generation (its documented lease-refresh behaviour, `20260908160000_offline_device_grants.sql`
  lines 1578–1622) but the `offline_allocation_ledger` row keeps the ORIGINAL `grant_id` /
  `generation`. `settle_offline_receipt()` requires `v_allocation.grant_id = receipt.grantId` and
  `v_allocation.generation = receipt.ticket.generation` (migration `20260909220000` lines 302–313), so
  every receipt naming the ticket exactly as the refreshed grant lists it is held with
  `reconciliation_required / evidence_ambiguous / reserved`; the ticket stays `allocated`, no shot is
  written, `offline_hold_count()` still counts it (2 with 0 scored), and redelivery replays the same
  hold. INFERRED (same predicate): the original-owner recovery path (deleted-and-recreated account
  re-issued its ticket under a new grant) is held the same way.
- Expected: a receipt bound to the grant that currently lists the ticket (same installation, ticket
  owned by the caller) settles once through `consume_offline_ticket()`; out-of-order receipts across
  generations remain safe.

### B2 (P2) — a transient `shot.session_not_found` is frozen into a permanent hold

- Repro: `ATTACK L9 (live DB)`.
- Observed: first delivery → `held / evidence_ambiguous / reserved` (the RPC maps
  `consume_offline_ticket() = 'shot.session_not_found'` to a durable hold, lines 339–346). After the
  session row exists the identical receipt returns `replayed` with the same hold; the ticket remains
  allocated (budget spent), the rating is never recorded.
- Expected: a receipt whose only defect is that its session has not synced yet is either not
  durably decided (so redelivery can settle) or the hold is resolvable once the session arrives.

### B3 (P2) — routine release-policy rotation strands every outstanding grant's receipts

- Repro: `ATTACK R1`.
- Observed: the route verifies the grant against the CURRENT `read_analysis_release_policy` row
  (`verifyOfflineExecutionGrant` requires canonical equality of `claims.release`, `offlineSignature.ts`
  line 630). After the policy rotates to a new version (same approval state, nothing withdrawn) a
  receipt for a grant issued and executed under the previous policy is handed to the RPC with
  `p_hold_reason = 'evidence_ambiguous'` and held permanently with the ticket reserved.
- Expected: the delayed receipt is judged against the release the grant was issued under (as the
  route already does for time — "evaluated as of the grant's last live instant"), or at least held
  with a reason that can be reconciled when the previous release was never withdrawn.

### B4 (P3) — `lifecycleSequence ≥ 2^31` is contract-valid at the edge and rejected by the RPC

- Repro: `ATTACK L8 (live DB)`.
- Observed: shared-types admits any positive safe integer; the RPC casts
  `(p_receipt ->> 'lifecycleSequence')::integer` (also `generation`), so the cast raises and the
  RPC answers `offline.invalid_input` — a non-durable rejection the device treats as terminal, never
  a hold.
- Expected: either the validators agree (both bound to int4) or the RPC parses with `bigint` and
  returns a durable verdict.
